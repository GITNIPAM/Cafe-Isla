# Storefront Surface Brief — Café Isla Home & Catalog

- **Scope & Visitor Mode**: Persuade (`resources/views/storefront/home.blade.php`, shared storefront header/footer, `/menu`, `/product/{slug}`)
- **Audience & Job**: Café patrons and coastal lifestyle shoppers discovering Café Isla's four Island Series beverages and Isla Merch capsule, choosing hot/iced pours or apparel sizes, and pouring items into a persistent bag.
- **Action & Proof**: Interactive 4-Island Bar switcher with 1-click Hot/Iced Add to Bag, Flora-style editorial split menu ledger, horizontal Isla Merch carousel, live Email Inbox proof drawer, and real sunlit 35mm-film drink & apparel imagery.

## Direction contract

- **THESIS**: Treats the home page as an editorial Philippine island café broadsheet and pour-bar ledger rather than a generic SaaS hero-over-grid storefront.
- **OWN-WORLD**: Sun-bleached abaca parchment (`#F6F1E6`), limestone split surfaces (`#ECE3D0`), ceremonial canopy deep green (`#1C3127`), espresso ink (`#261D18`), terracotta clay tag CTAs (`#C4553A`), crisp `0px` architectural edges, paired with `Libre Caslon Display` / `Libre Caslon Text` italics, tracked `Archivo` uppercase labels, and `Courier Prime` tasting-note coordinates and peso pricing.
- **STORY**: Visitors immediately recognize Café Isla as an authentic four-island café & coastal merch house, explore La Union's matcha bar alongside Siargao, Boracay, and El Nido pours, and add drinks or merch straight to their persistent bag.
- **FIRST VIEWPORT**: Slim terracotta announcement strip (`ISLA10`), clean open single-row header with SVG calligraphic wordmark & sun-wave emblem, a sharp `0px`-framed sunlit 16:9 coastal café bar hero photograph (zero overlaid promotional cards), followed directly by an asymmetric Caslon italic lead statement and interactive 4-Island Bar quick-pour selector.
- **FORM**: Editorial Café Broadsheet & Island Pour Ledger (Option 1 of 1), `seed: none (pinned) "Flora Wellness Cafe editorial print-zine island cafe direction"`.
